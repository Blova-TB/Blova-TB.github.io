Cybersecurity Course

Course taken during my first term at UQAC. It provided me with fundamental yet essential knowledge about computer security.

Takeaway

An Important Foundation

The teachings of this course allowed me to acquire a foundation in information security, as well as an overall vision of this field and its associated challenges. In my opinion, this is a crucial step for any developer, as it enables the establishment of best practices that greatly facilitate the securing of applications and data streams.

Core Topics

Key Skills and Concepts

Here are the main topics explored throughout this course:

  • Governance & Risk Management: CIA triad, threat modeling, and compliance frameworks (NIST, ISO 27001, GDPR).
  • Access Control: Authentication mechanisms (MFA, passwordless), authorization models (RBAC, ABAC), and Zero Trust architecture.
  • Security Operations (SOC): Implementation of defensive tools (SIEM, EDR, SOAR, IDS/IPS) and log analysis.
  • Attacks & Reconnaissance: OSINT techniques, attack life cycle (MITRE ATT&CK), lateral movement, and persistence.
  • Incident Response: Containment, remediation, workflow automation (Playbooks), and basic digital forensics.
  • AI Security: Vulnerabilities in generative models (Data Poisoning, prompt injection) and Explainable AI (XAI).
