Mission 1

OGI Project (Indemnity Management Tool)

The OGI project addressed a clear need: replacing an AS400 system in production (a technology dating back to 1988) with a modern solution, without interrupting service for the managers. The main difficulty was to make the existing system and the new platform coexist during the transition. To ensure smooth operation, I worked closely with the client's business teams to understand the existing processes.

My back-end role

On the Spring Boot side, I participated in creating and modifying RESTful APIs intended to expose and manipulate business data. The challenge was to provide reliable and secure services to support the implementation of the new architecture.

My front-end role

In Angular, I developed modular interfaces designed for real-world business use cases. Specifically, I implemented validators, such as REGEX expressions on sensitive fields (SIRET, E-CRAC, etc.), and set up auto-completion with data linked to the case being processed. This task required strong communication with the business teams.

Data migration

I also took part in the analysis and migration of data from AS400 to PostgreSQL. This phase required high vigilance and numerous checks to ensure data consistency.

Mission 2

AXOPEN Website with Next.js

In parallel with the OGI project, I contributed to AXOPEN's public showcase website. The goal was to establish a clear web presence consistent with the company's brand image. My objective was to deliver an effective UI/UX.

Showcase website development

I worked on adding pages and reusable sections, paying close attention to readability, visual consistency, and ease of maintenance.

Technical SEO constraint

The main constraint involved generating static pages with Next.js to optimize search engine ranking (SEO) while maintaining a clean connection to Strapi to populate the site from a CMS. This architecture combined performance, SEO, and editorial flexibility.
